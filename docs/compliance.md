# Evidence controls, sources and limits

Checked 2 November 2026. This is a documentation aid for audit practitioners, not an audit opinion or a certification of compliance.

## Recorded procedures

[ISA (NZ) 230, paragraphs 8 and 9](https://standards.xrb.govt.nz/standards-navigator/isa-nz-230/) addresses documenting procedures, results, evidence, specific items tested, preparer and review. Saved runs retain the population fingerprint, parameters, row identifiers, results, purpose, preparer and timestamp. A review adds the reviewer, conclusion, evidence reference and timestamp. The reviewer decides whether the evidence is sufficient. The NZ standard page also lists a new version for periods beginning on or after 15 December 2026. Review the applicable version for each engagement.

[ASA 230, paragraphs 8 and 9](https://standards.auasb.gov.au/asa-230-apr-2022) covers corresponding Australian documentation requirements. Names typed into this local tool are attribution, not authenticated identity or a digital signature.

## Control totals

CONTROL-TOTALS flags populations without matching independent row-count and signed-net controls. This is our data completeness check, not a named statutory threshold. Obtain the controls independently from the source system. Copying this tool's calculated totals back into the controls does not establish completeness. Count and net matching cannot prove every source transaction is correct.

## Review due

REVIEW-DUE flags saved tests without a review after their chosen due date. The due date is firm policy, not a legal deadline. A different reviewer is also our default firm policy, not a claim that paragraph 9 requires two people on every paper. Change it only through a documented, tested customisation.

## Preservation

Source populations, entries, runs, reviews and notes reject updates and deletes. Add a new snapshot or review to correct a record. This supports evidence preservation. It is not tamper-proof storage against a database owner. Source files remain the operator's responsibility. Set retention, access, backup and final-file assembly policies for the engagement. No automated retention deadline or legal sufficiency judgment is implemented.
